# Provenance

This portfolio presents my completed undergraduate analysis using university-provided simulated event data, helper functions and baseline methodology. The supplied material originated from [in2HEP](https://github.com/nikitapond/in2HEP), with teaching material acknowledging hackingEducation outreach resources.

My work comprises cut-threshold and direction studies, joint cut optimisation, configurable neural-network and hyperparameter studies, training-sample fraction experiments, variable-removal analysis, training-uncertainty studies and high-score-region diagnostics. The simple cut-based and dense-network baselines follow provided methodology. Retained model-building patterns are identified as adaptations rather than presented as independently invented architectures.

The university-provided datasets and helper implementations are omitted because this portfolio does not redistribute those supplied resources or assume permission to do so. Their historical roles in scaling, weighted sensitivity evaluation, adaptive binning and summary statistics are explained in the notebooks. No helper implementation or replacement is included, and no event-level data are redistributed.

Retained plots, aggregate tables and metrics are selected historical outputs from the completed analysis. They were not recomputed for this presentation. Illustrative code and method explanations accompany them, but the original execution environment, trained models and experiment state are not included; full reproducibility is not claimed.

Scientific limitations are described in the [README](README.md). This is an independent project, not an official ATLAS analysis. Attribution does not grant redistribution rights to omitted resources, and no repository-wide licence is assigned.
